Original source OBJ/MHCLO, native skeleton/weights and their notices are retained
in the evidence bundle. Source garment textures/materials are retained there;
the runtime shirt borrows the existing repository-original club kit atlas for
its pattern and fictional PQ badge, projected from the garment bind position.
Original garment OBJ UVs remain unchanged. The existing fictional number canvas
is composited afterward; no additional asset is imported. The player pool owns
the shared atlas; the shirt material borrows it without disposing it. Application
code licensing is separate from these CC0 asset declarations.

Original repository work includes the fixed production49 bind adapter, bounded
athletic torso adjustment, source-correspondence garment transfer and offset
transforms, generated render coverage mask and shared expression seam. The
coverage mask derives from original MHCLO body references and anatomical
adjacency, with original collar/cuff/hem footprints preserving openings. Its
position/index checksum guards the pinned body topology; all 87 seam vertices
remain visible. The existing fictional number canvas is projected onto the
skinned back-shirt material without adding an asset. Accepted
review-head attachments/morphs retain review-head-provenance.json. Unchanged
review skin, hair and brows retain review-player-provenance.json and
review-player-source-notices.txt. Existing
accepted footwear/cuff geometry and contact targets are reused unchanged.

Shorts/socks are material regions; this milestone supplies no new tailored lower
kit, boot model, goalkeeper garment/gloves, mocap or complete football motion
package. Original editable sources and maps are supplied; no .blend project is
claimed. Pose correction, collar refinement, angular outward-shoulder/underarm cloth
fold refinement and physical-device acceptance remain pending. Corrected
outward shoulder samples show no hole/body poke in their sampled cameras;
no global garment collision clearance is claimed. CPU transfer/seam diagnostics are not a collision certificate.
