Rules, ties and fees remain visible in all eight languages, in bold light text over a dark gradient. The room and private-group cards have decorative player/table scenes. These figures are illustrations, not claimed live participants. Pause applies to all introduction artwork; reduced-motion preferences remove animation, and offscreen or hidden-page artwork stops animating. The settled game renderers and all financial rules remain unchanged.

Chat repairs address the reported unreadable returned-entry notice and clipped gift card.
