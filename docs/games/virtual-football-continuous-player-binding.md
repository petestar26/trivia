## Body and fixed production rig

The pinned CC0 MakeHuman core `base.obj`, `default.mhskel` and `default_weights.mhw` come from commit `a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482`. The original source has 19,158 vertices, 13,380 anatomical body vertices and 26,756 anatomical triangles. Joint/helper surfaces are used to resolve native joint centers and then excluded from visible skin. The full original vertex index space is retained in `continuous-body-bind.json`; UV splitting and head clipping do not discard the correspondence needed by clothing.

The [body exporter](../../scripts/assets/build-football-continuous-body.py) resolves the 163 native bones and 139 weighted groups, then maps their weighted affine rest frames to the unchanged 49-joint production contact rig. It aligns shoulder/elbow/wrist and hip/knee/ankle endpoints, removes the source's arm spread and forward wrist bend, retargets segment lengths, constrains palm orientation using index-to-pinky spread, and maps all three segments of each native finger. Native weights are normalized, deliberately consolidated onto the production joints, capped to the greatest four influences and normalized again. Two anatomical source vertices lose weight under the cap; maximum discarded normalized mass is 0.008. Full normalized 49-joint weights are retained before the cap for garment transfer. Both GLBs contain actual skins, named joints and matching inverse bind matrices.

An original bounded athletic adjustment reduces the neutral foundation's anterior chest volume and broadens the waist. Chest adjustment fades within world Y 1.12–1.47 m and affects positive-Z volume beyond 0.07 m, with maximum reduction of 60% of `z - 0.07`. Waist width expands by at most 15% within Y 0.96–1.25 m. Both adjustments are weighted by trunk influences. The composed local derivative is retained in each source vertex's offset transform so the shirt follows the same shape adjustment. The exact upper-neck transform and production contact skeleton remain fixed. Source chest creases remain under the covered shirt region.

The clipped body export has 10,212 vertices and 18,440 triangles. Original anatomical source IDs, production positions, native-to-production offset transforms, full and capped weights, source UV corners and bone mapping remain editable JSON data in the evidence bundle. No `.blend` project or Blender exporter was used. Export tools use Python 3.9.6's standard library, glTF 2.0, and the source native rig/weight format version 110. The retained MakeClothes headers do not identify an exporter version.

## Preferred shirt transfer and original kit material

The runtime shirt is `toigo_basic_tucked_t-shirt`: MargaretToigo in the official pack listing, with `MRT` and `license CC0` in both retained OBJ and MHCLO headers. The supplied packet records the complete [official shirts01 CC0 archive](https://files.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/shirts01/shirts01_cc0.zip) and [listing](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/shirts01.html). Its other preview shirt is retained as an input candidate and is not the imported runtime garment. No new purchase or proprietary input is involved.

The [garment exporter](../../scripts/assets/build-football-continuous-garment.py) evaluates all 1,391 original MHCLO triples, including their signed coefficients and x/y/z scale directives. Each garment point is the coefficient-weighted body bind position plus the same transformed native offset. Weights interpolate the full normalized body influences, merge identical joints, clamp negative merged influence where needed, normalize, cap to four and normalize again. Forty-three source rows have a negative coefficient; two vertices require negative merged-weight clamping. Maximum discarded normalized weight under the four-influence cap is approximately 0.000415. All 8,100 polygon triangle corners retain their original source and UV identities; the export has 1,644 UV-split vertices and 2,700 triangles. The packet's static preview conversion is not used to bind the runtime shirt.

The runtime borrows the existing repository-original club kit atlas, preserving the club pattern and fictional PQ badge used by teammates. It projects that atlas from the shirt bind position with `u = 0.75 + atan2(x,z)/(2π)` and `v = 0.5 + 0.5*clamp((y-0.95)/0.57,0.0001,0.9999)`; the original garment OBJ UVs remain unchanged in the asset. Analytic angular gradients select the atlas mip level without a false filtering seam at the angular wrap. The existing fictional number canvas is composited afterward in bind space onto the back shirt material, so both kit markings and the number follow the garment skin rather than a separate rigid plane. The atlas remains owned by the player pool and is borrowed by the shirt material; disposing the actor does not dispose that shared texture. No additional asset is imported. Source shirt texture and material files remain in the editable packet; they are not imported into this runtime material. Existing review skin, hair and brow assets retain their unchanged [review provenance](../../apps/web/public/models/football/review-player-provenance.json) and source notices. Shorts and socks are material regions on the continuous anatomical surface, rather than new tailored garments. Existing accepted boot geometry and a short sock cuff are reused on the same skeleton. Source anatomical feet are hidden at the ankle transition; foot contacts, sole targets and rig dimensions are unchanged.

## Head seam and render coverage

The anatomical head source transform remains `[0.12*x, 1.50 + (y - 5.89)*0.12, 0.12*(z - 0.161)]`. The body holds it exactly for native Y ≥ 5.68. Both sides intersect the same source edges at world Y 1.53 m, copying the accepted head positions, normals and all eight relative position/normal morph targets before interpolation. Skin weights use `head = clamp((y - 1.49)/0.11, 0, 1)` with the remainder on `neck`. The lower neck continues jaw response with a smooth fade, and runtime body morph influences follow the head. Facial geometry, attachments, UVs and morph deltas above the join remain accepted head data; the retained head GLB is unchanged.

The preferred MHCLO contains **no `delete_verts` mask**. [continuous-player.ts](../../apps/web/src/components/football/engine/continuous-player.ts) therefore applies an original generated render-only mask to covered torso/upper-sleeve anatomy and anatomical feet. The [coverage generator](../../scripts/assets/build-football-continuous-coverage.py) uses 1,496 original body reference IDs from the preferred MHCLO, one anatomical topology adjacency ring, and the exact exported source-vertex correspondence. Original MHCLO collar/cuff/hem footprint loops (26/12+12/36 vertices) gate the mask around openings. This extends coverage through source-defined upper-back and proximal sleeve regions omitted by the earlier height rectangle while preserving exposed distal arms/hands and all 87 head-seam vertices. The earlier inward shoulder stress pose crossed the bare forearm in front of the shirt; that occlusion is not a garment-clipping defect. Outward shoulder abduction uses positive left and negative right Z rotation. The [generated helper](../../apps/web/src/components/football/engine/continuous-player-coverage.ts) contains a compact bitset for the pinned 10,212-vertex/55,320-index body and rejects a changed vertex count or position/index checksum. It retains the complete exported anatomical mesh and source binding data. This coverage policy is separate from collision fitting, and its current bounds live with the runtime implementation. Distant low-detail rendering retains the existing efficient fallback; the roster and match authority are unchanged.

## Reproduction and evidence

Run from the repository root with `PACKET` set to the extracted supplied `PlayQube_Football_Garment_Fit_Packet_20261020` directory, `NATIVE` set to the retained `source-foundation` directory containing `default.mhskel` and `default_weights.mhw`, and `OUT` set to a scratch output directory. The accepted head GLB and production contract are read from the repository. Neither command downloads an asset.

```sh
python3 scripts/assets/build-football-continuous-body.py \
  "$PACKET/sources/makehuman-core-base.obj" \
  "$NATIVE/default.mhskel" "$NATIVE/default_weights.mhw" \
  --output "$OUT" --glb
python3 scripts/assets/build-football-continuous-body.py \
  "$PACKET/sources/makehuman-core-base.obj" \
  "$NATIVE/default.mhskel" "$NATIVE/default_weights.mhw" \
  --output "$OUT" --glb --check
python3 scripts/assets/build-football-continuous-garment.py \
  --packet "$PACKET" --body-binding "$OUT/continuous-body-bind.json" \
  --output "$OUT"
python3 scripts/assets/build-football-continuous-coverage.py --evidence "$OUT"
python3 scripts/assets/build-football-continuous-coverage.py --evidence "$OUT" --check
cmp "$OUT/continuous-body.glb" apps/web/public/models/football/continuous-body.glb
cmp "$OUT/continuous-jersey.glb" apps/web/public/models/football/continuous-jersey.glb
```

The retained evidence bundle includes the original OBJ/MHCLO/native rig and weights, binding and garment correspondence maps, exporter reports, independent validation scripts/results and rendered evidence captured separately by the review workflow. Body regeneration with `--glb --check` passes. Independent shirt transfer checks cover every source row and topology corner with zero position, weight and UV transfer error. Independent head/body checks pass for 87 boundary vertices, all eight position/normal morph targets, 49 exact rest joints and 48 posed scale/jaw samples; maximum posed seam gap is `7.77e-8 m`. Those seam stress samples include inward shoulder rotations and do not constitute outward-abduction visual acceptance. Separate sparse CPU ray probes identify the inward-pose stripe as an opposing bare forearm crossing the shirt; with outward rotations, all 12 queried shoulder pixels have shirt hits and no visible body hits. Nine queried neutral-back pixels likewise have shirt hits and no body hits. These sparse probes are not a full-image collision clearance test. Coverage regeneration/check and two CPU regression tests additionally preserve the seam, hands and bare arms below the original cuff footprint while rejecting position/index/count changes. The focused continuous-player, pool and coverage suite passes all 36 tests. These results establish the stated transfer and seam properties; CPU probes and rendered evidence remain distinct from physical-device acceptance.

## Remaining artist work and acceptance

There is no finished non-destructive artist rig/corrective setup, tailored football shorts/socks, original boot model, goalkeeper long-sleeve garment or glove package in this milestone. No new mocap or complete football action package is delivered. Existing match/contact authority and original motion remain the basis of the actor.

Eight inner-elbow triangles fall below 5% of neutral area in the 120-degree stress probe. That extreme metric needs visual assessment and does not establish failure in natural bends. Corrected outward shoulder front/close browser samples show continuous skin-to-cuff coverage without an observed hole or body poke in those sampled cameras. A sharp outer-shoulder sleeve peak and underarm crease remain cloth tailoring/corrective weaknesses; these views do not establish global clearance. Historical inward-rotation shoulder frames are diagnostic errors retained separately from corrected outward frames. Extreme neck yaw stretches the shirt collar; collar/hem thickness, tailoring and pose correctives remain artist work. Unmasked full-body closest-triangle diagnostics include negative oriented gaps; they are potential clipping flags, not a global inside/outside test or collision certificate. Covered anatomy is deliberately masked at runtime.

This milestone does not claim console-quality realism, full-action collision clearance, physical-device acceptance or live deployment. The wider artist brief remains the finishing and acceptance reference.
