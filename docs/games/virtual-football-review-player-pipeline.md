## Review-player airborne kick recovery (2 November 2026)

The documented 73.155892 mm boot/ball overlap is reproducible in the current player lab at 0.34 s, with power 0.75, loft 0.2 and its **moving visible ball** at `(0, 0.151838921666, 0.2)`. It was not a stationary-ball-only test. The lab advances the ball at 5 m/s; the original free ankle initially advances faster and catches it after the strike. Limited actual-director samples use faster released balls and were already clear. Neither those samples nor this repair establish universal match collision clearance.

The repair changes the selected continuous review actor's **airborne kicking-leg path after contact**, rather than bending the shoe around the sphere. `poseKick` retains the legacy profile by default. Only the existing selected pool actor with a continuous player, and its corresponding developer lab, opt into `reviewFollowThrough`. The pool latches eligibility at the start of a kick or seek. Asynchronous appearance adoption during an existing kick cannot switch its pose; the next fresh kick can use the new profile. All other roster motion and the complete procedural fallback retain the original profile. The boot geometry, accepted clear-ball fitting, 55 mm retreat limit, dynamic uploads, foot/toe rest/bind, native jog and shared clips are unchanged.

For normalized post-strike progress `s`, the new original curve integrates
`v(s) = (3875/2141)*(1-s)^30 + (2548/2141)*(1-s^6)^2`.
Its integral over `[0,1]` is one, velocity decreases throughout, and endpoint values/speeds match the old curve: progress 0→1 and normalized velocity 3→0, with zero acceleration at the settled end. This brakes the initial strike speed sooner without reaccelerating the free foot. The curve is editable TypeScript; it is not imported motion capture or a physical impulse simulation. The inherited contact velocity discontinuity is not claimed repaired.

The contact instant remains 0.30 s, through duration 0.34 s, complete kick duration 0.85 s, and recovery endpoint unchanged. Full 49-bone world poses at/before strike and from the through endpoint onward, the planted leg throughout, rig binding, actor root, official/released ball facts and event/reveal clocks remain unchanged. Intermediate **airborne** ankle/foot/toe poses intentionally differ; old claims of universal post-contact free-foot pose equivalence do not describe this opt-in animation profile. The visual/posture adapter still solves its input contacts and the native jog remains confined to running.

Regressions include the actual moving-ball triangle clearance at both feet, scale/yaw variations, dense follow-through steps and numerical activation/release, unchanged default actors and active contacts, reduced motion, reset, ownership and resident dynamic buffers. Passing these measurements does not establish console realism, complete collision physics or physical-phone/live acceptance. Earlier geometry-only prototypes required pronounced proximal shoe bending and introduced large activation pops; they were rejected and are retained only as labelled failure evidence, not shipped code.
